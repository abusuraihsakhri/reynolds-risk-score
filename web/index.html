Reynolds Risk Score

Estimate 10-year risk of myocardial infarction, ischemic stroke, coronary revascularization, or cardiovascular death using sex-specific Reynolds models.

Educational/research use only. Not independently clinically validated software. Not a diagnostic result or treatment recommendation. Intended for adults without established cardiovascular disease; the male derivation cohort excluded diabetes. Interpretation should follow current local prevention guidance.

Estimated 10-year composite CVD risk

Enter the risk factors and select Calculate 10-year risk.

Units: Lipids in mg/dL, hsCRP in mg/L, blood pressure in mmHg. Values are not automatically converted.

Limitations: Derived from historical US cohorts; discrimination and calibration may differ across populations and current practice. hsCRP during acute illness can distort interpretation.

Privacy: All calculations occur within your browser. No clinical inputs are sent to a server, stored, or logged by this page.

Primary sources: Women (JAMA 2007) · Men (Circulation 2008)
